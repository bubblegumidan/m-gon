M-Gon Energy Systems

Energy Core

  • Energy Core: Stores power. Max energy scales with upgrades and abilities.
  • Regeneration: Base 1 unit/sec. Can be improved with green orbs or tech.
  • Orb Absorption: Converts nearby orbs into energy instantly.
  • Energy Barrier: Converts energy into a protective shield around your hitbox.
  • Energy Pulse: Radial damage wave consuming energy to attack multiple enemies.
  • Energy Overdrive: Triples attack damage while draining 1 energy/sec. Sparks fly out while active!
