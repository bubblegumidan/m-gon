M-Gon Energy Systems

1. Energy Core

The Energy Core stores your character's power. Maximum energy scales with upgrades, starting at 100.

Energy regenerates over time, at a base rate of 1 unit/sec, increased by certain green orbs or tech.

2. Energy Tech

  • Field Emitter: Unlocks the ability to analyze orb colors, discovering effects for your journey.
  • Energy Barrier: Converts 20 energy per second into a temporary shield that blocks projectiles.
  • Energy Pulse: Shoots a radial wave, consuming 15 energy and damaging enemies within range.
  • Orb Absorption: Absorbs nearby orbs automatically, converting them into energy at 1:1 ratio.

3. Energy Attacks

  • Laser Beam: Consumes 10 energy per shot, deals direct damage, can calculate advanced trajectory if upgraded.
  • Energy Drone: Deploys orbiting drones using 25 energy, they attack nearby enemies automatically.
  • Wormhole Pull: Consumes 50 energy, creates a wormhole pulling enemies and orbs into range.

4. Energy Interactions

Energy works with every ability. Some orbs enhance max energy, others boost regeneration. Certain tech combos can make energy almost infinite temporarily.

5. Key Notes

Always monitor energy levels before using high-cost abilities. Synergy between drones, lasers, and orb absorption can turn small energy pools into overwhelming power.
