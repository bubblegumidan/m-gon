M-Gon Manual

Abilities

  • Laser: Basic energy attack that damages enemies.
  • Orbital Drones: Ally drones circling your hitbox.
  • Super Balls Wave: Massive area attack that ricochets.

Energy Systems

  • Energy Core: Max energy 100, regenerates over time.
  • Field Emitter: Reveals orb effects.
  • Energy Pulse: Area attack consuming energy.

Lore

You are a bot made in a lab. Your soul slowly awakens as you travel through portals, gaining abilities, companions, and understanding of the orbs.

After 500 levels, you encounter the flashing exit door, and a final showdown with the arm occurs, teaching the lesson that friendship and creativity matter more than winning.
