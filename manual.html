M-Gon Manual

Abilities

  • Laser: Basic energy attack that damages enemies.
  • Orbital Drones: Ally drones circling your hitbox, attack enemies automatically.
  • Super Balls Wave: Massive area attack that ricochets off walls.
  • Wormhole: Pulls blocks and orbs toward you using energy.
  • Field Emitter: Reveals what orb colors do when collected.
  • Shield: Blocks attacks temporarily, consumes energy per second.
  • Clone/Mirror: Summons a short-lived clone mimicking your movements.

Energy Systems

  • Energy Core: Stores power, max energy scales with upgrades.
  • Regeneration: Base 1 unit/sec, improved with tech and green orbs.
  • Energy Pulse: Radial wave damaging enemies, consumes energy.
  • Energy Barrier: Converts energy into a protective shield.
  • Orb Absorption: Converts nearby orbs into energy.

Lore

You are a bot made in a lab, still connected to wires. Orbs represent data flowing into you, but you woke up early. Your journey takes you through bright blue portals with drones as companions, discovering abilities like Wormhole and Field Emitter.

After 500 levels, a flashing pink/red/blue door appears. You are pulled back by the arm before escape, but your soul awakens, gaining full abilities, health, and energy. The final lesson: the game is about friendships, creativity, and using limited resources to achieve everything.
