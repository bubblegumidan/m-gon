M-Gon Abilities

Offensive Abilities

  • Laser: Basic energy attack; deals damage to enemies.
  • Super Balls Wave: Area-of-effect projectile that ricochets off walls, hitting multiple targets.
  • Wormhole: Pulls blocks, enemies, and orbs toward you; uses energy while active.
  • Energy Pulse: Radial wave that damages all enemies nearby; consumes energy.
  • Field Emitter: Reveals orb properties; interacts with all orb types.

Orbital Drones

Ally drones orbit your hitbox, acting as both defensive and offensive units.

  • Scrap Drone: Rams enemies; 3 HP; damages on collision.
  • Foam Drone: Slows enemies on contact; small AoE effect.
  • Sound Drone: Emits sonic pulses that push back enemies.
  • General Orbital Drone: Circles your hitbox; attacks enemies automatically.

All drones follow the player and regenerate slowly over time if destroyed.

Defensive Abilities

  • Shield: Blocks enemy attacks temporarily; consumes energy per second.
  • Clone / Mirror: Creates a short-lived clone that mimics your movement for 2–3 seconds.
  • Size Manipulation: Shrink to dodge or grow to increase collision damage.
  • Speed Boost: Temporarily increases movement speed; works well with shrink.
  • Knockback / Repulsion: Pushes enemies away radially; scales with number of purple orbs.

Miscellaneous / Rare Effects

  • Jump / Gravity Alteration: Increases jump height or float time; allows sticking to walls briefly.
  • Time Slow: Slows the environment for tactical advantage; rare and limited duration.
  • Orb Multiplier / Combo: Increases the effect of other collected orbs for a short time.
